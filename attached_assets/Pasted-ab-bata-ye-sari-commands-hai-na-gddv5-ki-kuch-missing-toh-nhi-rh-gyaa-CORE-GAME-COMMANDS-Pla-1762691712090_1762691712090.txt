🎮 CORE GAME COMMANDS
Player Profile & Info
/start - Begin your journey in RealmShatter
/profile - View your complete player stats, level, class, and equipped gear
/stats - Detailed breakdown of HP, Attack, Defense, Break, Phase stats
/inventory or /inv - View all your items (weapons, armor, companions, resources)
/backpack - Manage inventory space and upgrades
Character & Class
/legacy or /class - View available Legacies (classes) and their abilities
/legacy select <name> - Choose your Legacy (Void Reaver, Aethermancer, etc.)
⚔️ COMBAT & EXPLORATION
Expeditions
/expedition or /explore - Go on an expedition (costs 10 stamina)
/expedition info - View current realm and available expeditions
Foraging
/forage - Harvest resources from the current realm (costs 5 stamina)
/resources - View your collected crafting materials
Fractures (Dungeons)
/fracture enter <tier> - Enter a Fracture dungeon (F1-F7)
/fracture list - View available Fractures and their rewards
/fracture info <tier> - Detailed info about a specific Fracture
🗡️ WEAPONS & GEAR
Sigil Arms (Weapons)
/sigilarm or /weapon - View your weapons
/sigilarm equip <name> - Equip a weapon
/sigilarm upgrade - Upgrade your equipped weapon
/sigilarm info <name> - View weapon details
Aegis Gear (Armor)
/aegis or /armor - View your armor pieces
/aegis equip <name> - Equip armor
/aegis upgrade - Upgrade your equipped armor
/aegis info <name> - View armor details
🐾 COMPANIONS
/companion or /companion list - View all your companions
/companion summon <name> - Summon/equip a companion
/companion train <name> - Train a companion to level up
/companion info <name> - View companion details and abilities
/companion feed <name> - Feed resources to boost companion
🏪 ECONOMY & CRAFTING
Shop & Market
/shop - Browse items for sale
/shop weapons - View weapons for sale
/shop armor - View armor for sale
/shop companions - View companion eggs for sale
/buy <item> - Purchase an item
/sell <item> - Sell an item from inventory
Crafting
/craft - View craftable items
/craft <item> - Craft a specific item
/forge - Fuse or upgrade items using materials
/awaken <companion> - Awaken a companion to unlock new abilities
🎰 RIFT TRIALS (Mini-games)
/rift - View available Rift Trial games
/rift flip - Play Rift Flip (coin flip, 49% win)
/rift roll - Play Ether Roll (dice game)
/rift slots - Play Shadow Slots (slot machine)
/rift spin - Play Astral Spin (wheel of fortune)
⚔️ PVP & COMPETITIVE
/pvp duel <@user> - Challenge another player to a duel
/pvp ranked - Enter ranked PvP match
/pvp ranks or /leaderboard - View PvP rankings
/pvp stats - View your PvP record (wins/losses)
🌍 REALMS & PROGRESSION
/realms - View all seven realms and your progress
/realm info <name> - Detailed info about a specific realm
/realm travel <name> - Travel to a different realm (if unlocked)
📖 LORE & STORY
/story - View main story progress
/lore <topic> - Read lore about realms, bosses, or the Shattering
/bestiary - View encountered enemies and bosses
/boss info <name> - View boss details and mechanics
💰 CURRENCY & RESOURCES
/balance or /wallet - View Luminite and Astral Shards
/daily - Claim daily Luminite and rewards (24hr cooldown)
/stamina - Check current stamina and regeneration
ℹ️ HELP & UTILITIES
/help - Full command guide
/guide <topic> - Specific guides (combat, companions, crafting, etc.)
/ping - Check bot response time
/settings - Adjust personal settings (notifications, display, etc.)
🎁 SPECIAL & EVENTS
/eclipse - Fight the Eclipsed Monarch (final boss, requires level 100)
/season - View current seasonal event
/achievements - View unlocked achievements
/titles - Manage earned titles and cosmetics
