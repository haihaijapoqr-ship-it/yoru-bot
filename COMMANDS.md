# 🌙 Yoru Bot - Command Guide

## 🎯 Two Ways to Use Commands!
Your bot supports both modern slash commands and classic text commands:

**Slash Commands (Modern):**
- Type `/` in Discord to see all commands
- Auto-complete for parameters
- Professional appearance
- Example: `/kiss @user` or `/profile`

**Text Commands (Classic):**
- Use the `yoru` prefix
- Example: `yoru kiss @user` or `yoru profile`

Choose whichever you prefer - both work identically!

## Getting Started
Type `yoru start` or `/start` to create your profile and begin your journey in the Hackverse!

## 🎮 Game Commands
Build your hacker empire in the cyberpunk world:

### Resource Management
- `yoru hack` - Hack nodes for credits and fragments (costs 1⚡, 10s cooldown)
- `yoru scan` - Scan the network for artifacts (costs 2⚡, 15s cooldown)  
- `yoru work` - Do jobs for credits and energy (20s cooldown)
- `yoru daily` - Claim daily rewards (24 hour cooldown)

### Your Profile
- `yoru profile` or `yoru p` - View your stats, level, and resources
- `yoru inventory` or `yoru inv` - Check your weapons and items

## 💰 Shop & Economy
- `yoru shop` - Browse available weapons
- `yoru buy <number>` - Purchase a weapon (example: `yoru buy 1`)
- `yoru equip <weapon name>` - Equip a weapon you own

### Available Weapons
1. ⚪ **Common** - Neon Dagger (100₿, +2 ATK), Data Blade (150₿, +3 ATK)
2. 🔵 **Rare** - Cyber Katana (300₿, +5 ATK), Plasma Sword (400₿, +6 ATK)
3. 🟣 **Epic** - Phantom Blade (800₿, +10 ATK), Void Slicer (1000₿, +12 ATK)
4. 🟡 **Legendary** - Yoru's Edge (2500₿, +20 ATK), Infinity Breaker (3500₿, +25 ATK)

## 📊 Resources
- **₿ Credits** - Main currency for buying items
- **💠 Fragments** - Crafting materials (future use)
- **⚡ Energy** - Needed for hack and scan commands (max 10)
- **✨ XP** - Experience points to level up
- **❤️ Emotion XP** - Gained from social interactions

## 💡 Tips
1. Start with `yoru daily` for instant resources
2. Use `yoru work` to recover energy when you're low
3. Social commands give free XP - interact with friends!
4. Save up for Epic/Legendary weapons for maximum power
5. Energy regenerates with daily and work commands
